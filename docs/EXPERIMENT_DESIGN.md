# Frozen experiment design

Objective: assess whether one six-layer reference architecture can reuse a controller, observer, packet contract, monitor, logging and verification implementation across native F1TENTH Gym and MetaDrive plants. This is software-in-the-loop evidence, with a simulated plant substituting for the future Physical Layer. No physical Digital Twin, ROS 2/DDS transport, HIL, camera perception or real-world safety claim is evaluated.

The task is geometric centerline following using pure pursuit and synthetic noisy pose, heading and speed measurements. Circle, ellipse and wavy tracks have matched dimensions in wheelbases. Two speeds are scaled by the square root of wheelbase ratio to match reference lateral acceleration; this does not establish full dynamic similarity. Native physics steps are 1 ms for F1TENTH and 5 ms for MetaDrive, with a 5 ms event grid, 20 ms controls and 50 ms source sampling. Native states are never overwritten after initialization.

## Calibration and held-out evaluation
Calibrate the yaw gain and 99.9th-percentile absolute-residual threshold (floor 0.05 rad/s) on circle and ellipse only, two speeds, seeds 20/21/22: 12 runs per backend. Freeze the fitted models before evaluation. The wavy geometry is entirely absent from calibration. Evaluate all three geometries, two speeds and six disjoint seeds in campaigns A (300/301/302) and B (600/601/602). These are controlled finite test matrices, not independent samples of road traffic.

Per backend and campaign: E0 has 54 nominal direct/timestamp-aware/stale-feedback runs; E1 has 36 runs at 75 ms observation delay with timestamp-aware or stale feedback; E2 has 18 runs with a 15% observer-wheelbase mismatch; E3 has 108 runs covering steering biases -8/-2/+2/+8 degrees, 20% independently sampled packet loss and a 0.20 s packet-loss burst. Total: 864 primary runs and 24 calibration runs. The zero-delay endpoint is covered by E0. No controller or threshold tuning is permitted using evaluation results.

## Diagnostic contract
The yaw monitor receives delivered noisy measurements and intended commands only. For each five-packet source-time window, it integrates linearly interpolated measured speed times piecewise-held intended curvature tan(steering)/wheelbase, and divides by window duration. Heading change over that same source-time interval provides measured mean yaw rate. The residual subtracts a calibration-only scalar-gain prediction. Three consecutive residual exceedances produce a general alarm. A fault-attributed detection requires three consecutive exceeding windows whose start timestamps are all at or after the declared fault onset. This avoids crediting pre-fault measurements. General pre-fault/nominal alarms are reported separately. A two-second intended-command history supports stretched dropout windows; unsupported windows invalidate/reset the monitor rather than fabricate predictions. Source age greater than 80 ms triggers a separate freshness alarm. Freshness is a packet-timing check, not steering-fault classification.

## Outcomes and limitations
Report circuit completion, sampled corridor departures (half-width 0.75 wheelbases), tracking RMSE/p95, state disagreement versus native truth, observer correction effects, steering-fault detections/misses, attribution latency, nominal alarms and freshness alarms. Ground truth is used for initialization and scoring, never diagnostic prediction. Exclude the first two scaled seconds from performance summaries. Fault starts after four scaled seconds, rounded to the source grid. Report closed-loop tracking separately from same-trajectory observer disagreement; improved state agreement does not imply improved control.

Pair conditions by geometry/speed/seed/campaign. Exploratory bootstrap intervals resample complete seed blocks (six blocks per backend), not individual time samples. Fault outcomes are finite-matrix counts and ranges across seed blocks, not road-level failure probabilities. Report wavy-geometry outcomes separately. Add six native half-timestep comparisons and two fresh-environment reruns as numerical/reproduction checks, not primary replicates.
